## 1. Client services agreement (contract template)

**VIDEO EDITING SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made between **[Editor / Business name]** ("Editor") and **[Client / Company name]** ("Client"), effective **[date]**.

**1. Services and deliverables.** The Editor will provide: **[describe the project]**. Deliverables: **one (1) final video** up to **[length]**, in **[resolution]**, delivered as **[file format]**, in **[number of aspect ratios]**. Anything not explicitly listed here is out of scope.

**2. Revisions.** This project includes **two (2) rounds of revisions**. Each additional round is billed at **$[X] per round** (or **$[Y]/hour**) and is payable before work resumes.

**3. Change requests / out of scope.** Any work beyond Section 1 requires a **written change order** billed at **$[Y]/hour**. Work begins once the change order is approved and, if applicable, paid.

**4. Project and source files.** The Client receives the **final exported video only**. Native project files, raw footage, and assets remain the Editor's property and are available for an additional **$[Z]**.

**5. Fees and payment.** Total fee: **$[total]**. A **non-refundable deposit of [50%]** is due before work begins; the balance is due **before final files are delivered**. Late payments accrue **[1.5%] per month**. Final files are released only after payment in full. 🔶

**6. Cancellation / kill fee.** If the Client cancels after work has begun, the deposit is retained and completed work is billed pro-rata. 🔶

**7. Timeline.** First cut by **[date]**, provided the Client supplies all materials by **[date]**. Delays in Client materials or feedback extend deadlines accordingly.

**8. Client responsibilities.** The Client provides footage, brand assets, and feedback within **[3] business days** of each delivery.

**9. Intellectual property and licensing.** Upon payment in full, the Client receives rights to use the **final delivered video** for **[agreed use]**. The Editor may display the work in their portfolio unless agreed otherwise in writing. The Editor warrants the edit is original work; the **Client is responsible for the rights/licenses** to any footage, music, fonts, or logos they supply. 🔶

**10. Limitation of liability.** The Editor's total liability is limited to the fees paid. No liability for indirect or consequential damages. 🔶

**11. Confidentiality.** Both parties keep confidential information private.

**12. Independent contractor.** The Editor is an independent contractor responsible for their own taxes.

**13. Governing law.** Governed by the laws of **[state / country]**. 🔶

**14. Entire agreement.** This is the entire agreement; changes must be in writing and signed.

Signed — **Editor:** ___________ Date: ____ · **Client:** ___________ Date: ____

## 7. Scope-creep playbook (8 scenarios, scripted)

- **"Can you also just add…?"** → "Happy to! That's outside our current scope, so I'll send a quick change order at **$[Y]/hour** and start once approved."
- **"It's a tiny change."** → "Totally — small changes still count as a revision round. We're on round [2 of 2]; after this they're **$[X]/round**. Proceed?"
- **"Can I get the project files?"** → "Sure — final export is included; native project files are an add-on at **$[Z]**. Add them?"
- **"Can you rush it?"** → "I can prioritize it for a **+[25%]** rush fee. Want me to bump it to the front?"
- **"I'll pay after it performs well."** → "I keep it simple: [50%] to start, balance before final delivery — protects us both."
- **"My friend can do it cheaper."** → "Totally understand! My rate reflects [turnaround/quality/revisions included]. If budget's tight, I can scope a smaller package."
- **"Can you send the raw cut so we can finish it?"** → "I deliver the final export; partial/native files are an add-on at **$[Z]** since they include my project work."
- **"We need 5 more versions for other platforms."** → "Great — each extra aspect-ratio version is **$[per version]**. Want all five? I'll add them to the invoice."

## 🎁 Bonus 1 — Pricing & packaging guide

**Ways to price:** per project (best for clients), per finished minute, per deliverable, day rate (shoots), or monthly retainer (best for you — predictable income).

**Set your base:** target monthly income ÷ realistic billable hours = your hourly floor. Quote *projects*, but never quote below that floor.

**Package good / better / best (anchoring):**
- **Essential** — 1 video, [length], 1 aspect ratio, 1 revision.
- **Pro (most popular)** — 1 video, captions + light motion graphics, 2 aspect ratios, 2 revisions.
- **Premium** — multi-video or long-form + B-roll sourcing + thumbnail + 3 platform versions.

> *Tip:* most buyers pick the middle. Make "Pro" the option you most want to sell.
