## 1. Client services agreement (contract template)

> *Nota:* questo è il cuore del prodotto. Impaginalo in Google Docs + Word + Canva (3 formati). 🔶 = clausola da far rivedere a un avvocato prima di vendere.

**VIDEO EDITING SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made between **[Editor / Business name]** ("Editor") and **[Client / Company name]** ("Client"), effective **[date]**.

**1. Services and deliverables.** The Editor will provide: **[describe the project]**. Deliverables: **one (1) final video** up to **[length]**, in **[resolution]**, delivered as **[file format]**, in **[number of aspect ratios]**. Anything not explicitly listed here is out of scope.

**2. Revisions.** This project includes **two (2) rounds of revisions**. Each additional round is billed at **$[X] per round** (or **$[Y]/hour**) and is payable before work resumes.

**3. Change requests / out of scope.** Any work beyond the deliverables in Section 1 requires a **written change order** billed at **$[Y]/hour**. Work begins once the change order is approved and, if applicable, paid.

**4. Project and source files.** The Client receives the **final exported video only**. Native project files, raw footage, and project assets remain the property of the Editor and are available for an additional **$[Z]**.

**5. Fees and payment.** Total project fee: **$[total]**. A **non-refundable deposit of [50%]** is due before work begins; the balance is due **before final files are delivered**. Late payments accrue **[1.5%] per month**. Final files are released only after payment in full. 🔶

**6. Cancellation / kill fee.** If the Client cancels after work has begun, the deposit is retained and any completed work is billed pro-rata. 🔶

**7. Timeline.** The Editor will deliver the first cut by **[date]**, provided the Client supplies all required materials by **[date]**. Delays in Client materials or feedback extend deadlines accordingly.

**8. Client responsibilities.** The Client provides footage, brand assets, and timely feedback (within **[3] business days** of each delivery). Repeated delays may pause the project.

**9. Intellectual property and licensing.** Upon payment in full, the Client receives the rights to use the **final delivered video** for **[agreed use]**. The Editor may display the work in their portfolio unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. The Editor warrants the edit is their original work; the **Client is responsible for holding the rights/licenses** to any footage, music, fonts, or logos they supply. 🔶

**10. Limitation of liability.** The Editor's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid by the Client. The Editor is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. 🔶

**11. Confidentiality.** Each party will keep the other's confidential information private and use it only to perform this Agreement.

**12. Independent contractor.** The Editor is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Client. The Editor is responsible for their own taxes.

**13. Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[state / country]**. 🔶

**14. Entire agreement.** This is the entire agreement between the parties. Any change must be in writing and signed by both.

Signed:
**Editor:** _______________  Date: ______
**Client:** _______________  Date: ______

## 4. Client onboarding questionnaire

> *Nota:* il cliente lo manda al SUO cliente prima di iniziare. Riduce le revisioni del 50%.

1. What is this video for, and where will it be published? (platform, audience)
2. What's the goal — what should viewers do or feel?
3. Desired final length and aspect ratio(s)?
4. Hard deadline / launch date?
5. Where is the footage, and how will you send it? (Drive, Frame.io, Dropbox)
6. Do you have a brand kit (logo, fonts, colors)? Please attach.
7. Music: do you have a licensed track, or should the editor suggest royalty-free options?
8. Any reference videos you love (links)? Anything to avoid?
9. Who gives final approval, and how fast can you give feedback?
10. Captions/subtitles needed? Which language(s)?

## 7. Scope-creep playbook (what to say)

- **"Can you also just add…?"** → "Happy to! That's outside our current scope, so I'll send a quick change order at **$[Y]/hour** and start once it's approved."
- **"It's a tiny change."** → "Totally — small changes still count as a revision round. We're on round [2 of 2]; after this they're **$[X]/round**. Want me to proceed?"
- **"Can I get the project files?"** → "Sure — the final export is included; native project files are an add-on at **$[Z]**. Want me to add them?"
- **"Can you rush it?"** → "I can prioritize it for a **+[25%]** rush fee. Want me to bump it to the front?"
- **"I'll pay you after it performs well."** → "I keep things simple: [50%] to start, balance before final delivery. That protects us both and keeps your project on schedule."
