**Cover:** **The Video Editor's Scope-Creep Shield** — 5 clauses that stop "just one more revision" from killing your profit.

**Clauses (use this wording):**
1. **Revision limit:** "This project includes **two (2) rounds of revisions**. Each additional round is **$[X]**, payable before work resumes."
2. **Define done:** "Deliverables: **one (1) final video**, [length], [resolution], [number of aspect ratios]. Anything not listed is not included."
3. **Out of scope:** "Work beyond the deliverables requires a **written change order** at **$[Y]/hour**."
4. **Project files:** "The client receives the **final export only**. Native project files are available for **$[Z]**."
5. **Deposit + kill fee:** "A **non-refundable deposit of [50%]** is due before work begins; balance before final delivery."
