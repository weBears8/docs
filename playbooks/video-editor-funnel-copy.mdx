## 1. Lead magnet (testo completo del PDF gratuito)

> *Nota:* impagina questo in Canva come PDF di 4–6 pagine. È il regalo che cattura l'email.

**Titolo / cover:**
> **The Video Editor's Scope-Creep Shield**
> 5 clauses that stop "just one more revision" from killing your profit.

**Intro:**
> You took the project for $X. Three weeks and nine "tiny" revisions later, you're working for $4/hour and the client still isn't happy. Scope creep is the #1 reason freelance video editors burn out and underearn. These 5 clauses — in plain English — draw the line *before* the project starts. Copy the wording, adapt it to your business, and never work for free again.
>
> *(This guide is educational, not legal advice — see the note at the end.)*

**Clause 1 — The revision limit.**
> *The problem:* "endless revisions" with no cap. *The fix:* include a set number of rounds; bill the rest.
> *Use this:* "This project includes **two (2) rounds of revisions**. Each additional round is billed at **$[X] per round**, payable before work resumes."

**Clause 2 — Define "done" (deliverables & format).**
> *The problem:* the client suddenly expects 5 aspect ratios and captions. *The fix:* spell out exactly what they get.
> *Use this:* "Deliverables: **one (1) final video**, up to **[length]**, in **[resolution]**, in **[number of aspect ratios]**. Anything not listed is not included."

**Clause 3 — Out-of-scope = change order.**
> *The problem:* "can you also just…" requests that balloon the job. *The fix:* new work = written change order at your rate.
> *Use this:* "Work beyond the deliverables requires a **written change order** billed at **$[Y]/hour**, starting once approved."

**Clause 4 — Project & source files (the big one).**
> *The problem:* clients demand your raw project files for free. *The fix:* they get the final export; project files are a paid add-on.
> *Use this:* "The client receives the **final exported video only**. Native project files remain the Editor's property and are available for **$[Z]**."

**Clause 5 — Deposit, payment & kill fee.**
> *The problem:* ghosting and late payment. *The fix:* non-refundable deposit + balance before delivery.
> *Use this:* "A **non-refundable deposit of [50%]** is due before work begins; the balance is due **before final files are delivered**."
