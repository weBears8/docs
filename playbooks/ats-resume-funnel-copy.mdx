## 1. Lead magnet (PDF gratuito)

**Cover:** **ATS Resume Checklist** — 12 fixes that get you past the bot and in front of a human.

**Intro:** Up to 75% of resumes are filtered by software before a person ever sees them. Most rejections aren't about you — they're about formatting and keywords. Fix these 12 things and your resume actually gets read.

**The 12 fixes (checklist):**
1. Single column, no tables or text boxes.
2. Standard headings (Summary, Experience, Skills, Education).
3. Target job title near the top.
4. Match exact keywords from the job post.
5. Spell out + abbreviate key terms once (e.g., "Project Management (PMP)").
6. Common font (Arial/Calibri).
7. No images, icons, or skill bars.
8. No key info in headers/footers.
9. 3 quantified wins in the top third.
10. Consistent date format.
11. Save in the requested format (.docx/PDF).
12. Proofread — small errors get you cut.

**Soft CTA:** Want the full kit — templates, **keyword banks for your industry**, a bullet library with metrics, cover letter, and LinkedIn frameworks? Get **The ATS Resume Kit** → (link). *Educational; no job guaranteed.*
