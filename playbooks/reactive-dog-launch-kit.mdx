## 3. Reel/TikTok — 7 script (15–25 sec)

1. **Relatable:** "POV: you see a dog across the street and your heart drops. The one word that changed our walks: *threshold*. Free guide in bio — force-free only."
2. **Teach:** "Stop correcting the lunge. Reactivity is fear, and you can't punish fear away. Do this: stay far enough that your dog can still eat, then pay the trigger."
3. **Hope:** "Week 1 vs week 6 with a reactive dog. No prong, no shock — just distance + treats + a plan."
4. **U-turn:** "What to do the second a trigger gets too close: cue, scatter treats, walk away. Distance first."
5. **Home:** "Walk reactivity often starts at home. 3 ways to lower your dog's baseline today."
6. **Gear:** "Force-free gear that actually helps a reactive dog (and what to avoid)."
7. **Mindset + CTA:** "A setback isn't failure — it's data. Grab the free Threshold Trick in bio."

- **Reddit:** r/reactivedogs, r/Dogtraining, r/OpenDogTraining — **solo force-free**, dai valore, niente vendite dirette; linka in commento/bio dove permesso.
- **Facebook groups:** "Reactive Dogs Support", gruppi force-free / positive reinforcement, gruppi di razza.
- **TikTok/Instagram:** #reactivedog #reactivedogtraining #forcefreetraining — commenta utile sotto i video.
- **Strategy:** 80% empatia/valore, 20% offerta. Costruisci la **lista email** (l'asset).

- **Opt-in 25–30%+** = gancio ok · **3–10 vendite** dai primi ~150–300 lead = espandi · **Email→vendita 2–5%** realistico.
- Opt-in alto + 0 vendite → sistema offerta/prezzo. Opt-in basso → cambia gancio/traffico.
- **Pre-sell** ("Pre-order — delivered in 7 days") = validazione a rischio minimo.
