## 1. Task 2 — the reusable skeleton (works on any topic)

> *Nota:* questa è la struttura a 4 paragrafi che vale per quasi tutti i temi. Insegna al cliente a *riempire*, non a memorizzare.

- **Introduction (2 sentences):** Paraphrase the question + state your position/roadmap.
  > *"It is often argued that [paraphrase of topic]. While some believe [view A], this essay will argue that [your position]."*
- **Body 1 (4–5 sentences):** Topic sentence → explain → example → link.
  > *"One major reason for this is that [point]. This means that [explain]. For instance, [example]. As a result, [link back to position]."*
- **Body 2 (4–5 sentences):** Second reason, same shape (point → explain → example → link).
- **Conclusion (2 sentences):** Restate position + summary.
  > *"In conclusion, although [other view], the evidence suggests that [your position], mainly because [reason 1] and [reason 2]."*

**The 5 Task 2 question types (each with a one-line approach):**
1. **Opinion (agree/disagree):** pick ONE clear side; both bodies support it.
2. **Discussion (both views + opinion):** Body 1 = view A, Body 2 = view B, state your opinion throughout/conclusion.
3. **Problem/Solution (or Cause/Solution):** Body 1 = problem/cause, Body 2 = solution.
4. **Advantages/Disadvantages:** Body 1 = advantages, Body 2 = disadvantages; address whether they outweigh.
5. **Two-part question:** Body 1 answers Q1, Body 2 answers Q2 directly.

## 2. Task 1 Academic — the overview-first template

> *Nota:* la chiave del Band 7 in Task 1 è un **overview chiaro** (le tendenze principali, senza numeri).

- **Introduction (1 sentence):** paraphrase what the visual shows.
  > *"The [chart/graph/table] illustrates [what] [where] over [time/period]."*
- **Overview (1–2 sentences, no data):** the 2 biggest trends.
  > *"Overall, it is clear that [biggest trend], while [second trend]."*
- **Detail paragraph 1:** group and describe the main figures with data + comparison.
- **Detail paragraph 2:** the remaining figures, grouped logically.

**Quick frames by visual type:**
- **Line graph (change over time):** "rose/fell/peaked/plateaued at [X] in [year]."
- **Bar chart / table (comparison):** "the highest was [X], compared with [Y]; [A] was roughly twice [B]."
- **Pie chart (proportion):** "[X] accounted for [%], the largest share."
- **Process diagram:** "First… Next… After that… Finally…" (passive voice: "the [item] is [verb-ed]").
- **Map (change over time):** "a [building] was built where the [field] had previously been."

**General Training letters** (Task 1 GT): formal / semi-formal / informal openers, body, and sign-offs, with the 3 bullet points always covered.

## 3. High-band phrase bank (by function)

- **Paraphrasing:** "it is often argued that…", "a common view is that…", "many people believe…"
- **Adding/sequencing:** "furthermore", "in addition", "moreover", "first and foremost"
- **Cause/effect:** "as a result", "consequently", "this leads to", "owing to"
- **Contrast:** "however", "on the other hand", "despite this", "whereas"
- **Examples:** "for instance", "a clear example of this is", "to illustrate"
- **Opinion:** "I would argue that", "from my perspective", "it seems reasonable to suggest"
- **Describing trends (Task 1):** "rose steadily", "fell sharply", "remained stable", "fluctuated", "reached a peak of"
- **Concluding:** "in conclusion", "to sum up", "the evidence suggests that"

> *Tip:* usare 1–2 phrase per funzione, non infilarne troppe (gli esaminatori penalizzano l'uso meccanico).

## 5. Self-assessment checklist (i 4 band descriptor)

- **Task Achievement / Response:** ho risposto a TUTTE le parti? Posizione chiara? (Task 1: overview presente?)
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** paragrafi logici? linking words usati bene (non meccanici)?
- **Lexical Resource:** vocabolario vario e preciso? paraphrasing senza copiare la domanda?
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** mix di frasi semplici e complesse? pochi errori che non bloccano la comprensione?

> Spunta tutte e 4 prima di considerare "finito" un saggio di pratica.

## 6. "Band 6.5 to 7" fixes (i piccoli cambi che spostano il punteggio)

- Aggiungi un **overview** chiaro in Task 1 (causa n.1 del 6.5).
- **Rispondi a tutte le parti** della domanda in Task 2 (una posizione chiara, sostenuta).
- Smetti di **copiare le parole della domanda** → parafrasa.
- **Estendi le idee** (point → explain → example), non elencarle.
- Riduci i **linking words meccanici**; varia.
- Controlla **articoli (a/the), preposizioni e accordi** — gli errori piccoli ma frequenti pesano.
