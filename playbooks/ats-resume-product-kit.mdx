## 1. ATS rules — what actually beats the bot

**Do:**
- Use a **single-column, standard layout** (ATS misreads tables/columns/text boxes).
- Use **standard section headings**: Summary, Experience, Skills, Education.
- **Match keywords** from the job post (titles, hard skills, tools) — exactly as written.
- Use a **common font** (Arial, Calibri) and save as **.docx or PDF** as requested.
- Spell out **and** abbreviate key terms once: "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)."
- Put **dates and job titles** in plain text, consistent format.

**Don't:**
- No headers/footers for key info (ATS often skips them), no images/icons/logos, no graphics-only skill bars.
- No fancy templates with columns/tables. No uncommon section names ("Where I've Made Magic").
- Don't keyword-stuff invisibly — modern ATS and recruiters flag it.

> **The 6-second scan:** after the ATS, a human skims for ~6 seconds. Top third must show: target title, a 2-line summary, and 3 quantified wins.

## 2. Resume template (structure)

```
[FULL NAME]
[Target job title] · [City, Country] · [email] · [phone] · [LinkedIn URL]

SUMMARY
[2–3 lines: who you are + years + 1–2 quantified strengths + target role]

CORE SKILLS
[8–12 keywords/hard skills, comma or bullet list — pulled from the job post]

EXPERIENCE
[Job title] — [Company], [City] · [dates]
- [Achievement bullet: action + what + metric/result]
- [Achievement bullet]
- [Achievement bullet]

EDUCATION
[Degree], [Institution] · [year]

CERTIFICATIONS / TOOLS (optional)
[relevant licenses, software]
```

## 4. Achievement bullet library (action + metric + result)

**Formula:** `[Strong verb] + [what you did] + [number/metric] + [result].`

- "Increased [X] by **[%]** by [doing Y]."
- "Reduced [cost/time/errors] **[%/amount]** through [action]."
- "Managed a **$[amount]** budget / a team of **[n]**."
- "Delivered [project] **[time]** ahead of schedule, saving **$[amount]**."
- "Grew [metric] from **[A] to [B]** in **[period]**."

**Power verbs:** led, built, launched, increased, reduced, streamlined, negotiated, designed, implemented, improved, automated, mentored, delivered, scaled, optimized.

## 5. Summary templates by level

- **Entry / new grad:** "Motivated [field] graduate with [internship/project] experience in [skills], seeking a [role] to apply [strength]."
- **Mid:** "[Title] with [X] years in [field], known for [quantified win]. Skilled in [3 keywords], targeting [role]."
- **Senior:** "[Title] with [X]+ years leading [scope]. Delivered [big quantified result]; expertise in [domain]."
- **Career-changer:** "[Background] professional transitioning to [new field], bringing transferable [skills] plus [new training/cert]; eager to [contribute]."

## 6. Cover letter template

> Dear [Hiring Manager], I'm excited to apply for the **[role]** at **[company]**. In my [X] years as a **[title]**, I [quantified achievement relevant to the role]. I'm drawn to [company] because [specific reason]. I'd bring [2 relevant strengths] and a track record of [result]. I'd welcome the chance to discuss how I can help [team/goal]. Thank you for your time. — [Name]

**Career-changer version:** open with *why* the switch + transferable wins:
> "After [X] years in [old field], I'm moving into [new field] because [reason]. The skills that made me effective — [transferable skills] — map directly to [role]: for example, [quantified example]."

## 7. LinkedIn frameworks

- **Headline:** "[Target title] | [specialty] | [result or value]" — e.g., "Registered Nurse | Acute Care | Reduced falls 30%."
- **About (4 lines):** who you are + what you do + a proof point + a call to connect/hire.

## 8. Post-interview follow-up email

## 🎁 Bonus 1 — "Tailor in 10 minutes" keyword-matching method

Paste the job post + your resume side by side. Highlight every **hard skill, tool, and title** in the post; make sure the exact terms appear in your Skills + Experience (where true). That single habit is what gets you past the ATS.

## 🎁 Bonus 2 — The 6-second scan checklist

- [ ] Target job title near the top? · [ ] 2-line summary? · [ ] 3 quantified wins in the top third?
- [ ] Single column, standard headings? · [ ] No images/tables/headers for key info? · [ ] Saved in the requested format?

## 🎁 Bonus 3 — Salary negotiation email (after an offer)

> Subject: [Role] offer — quick question
> Hi [Name], thank you so much for the offer — I'm thrilled. Based on my experience with [quantified value] and market rates for this role, I was hoping we could discuss a base closer to **$[target]**. I'm confident I'll deliver [result]. Is there flexibility? — [Name]
