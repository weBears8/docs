## 2. Primo traffico — contenuti pronti

> *Nota:* il pubblico è internazionale e si trova su **r/IELTS, YouTube/TikTok, e gruppi Facebook IELTS per Paese** (India, Nigeria, Filippine, Vietnam, ecc.). Dai valore, niente spam; rispetta le regole della community.

**A) Post utile per r/IELTS (dai valore)**
> **Title:** The structure fix that finally moved my Writing from 6.5 to 7
> **Body:** I kept getting 6.5 and thought I needed harder vocabulary. Wrong. Two structural fixes did it: (1) a clear position stated in the intro and held through both body paragraphs in Task 2, and (2) a proper **overview** (main trends, no numbers) in Task 1. I turned my approach into a reusable 4-paragraph skeleton — happy to share it as a free 1-pager if that's allowed here. What moved *your* score the most?

**B) 3 script per reel/TikTok/Shorts (15–25 sec)**
> **Reel 1 (pain):** "Stuck at IELTS Writing 6.5? It's probably not your vocabulary — it's structure. Here's the 4-line intro that examiners reward. Free skeleton in bio."
> **Reel 2 (teach):** "Task 1 secret: write the **overview** first — the 2 biggest trends, no numbers. That one line is a Band 7 requirement most people skip."
> **Reel 3 (templates):** "One essay structure for ANY Task 2 topic: paraphrase + position → reason + example → reason + example → restate. Full template pack in bio."

**C) 5 hook per caption / commento**
1. "6.5 in Writing? It's structure, not vocabulary."
2. "The Task 1 'overview' line that's worth half a band."
3. "One Task 2 skeleton for any topic 👇"
4. "Stop copying the question — paraphrase like this."
5. "Free: a reusable Band 7+ essay template."

'IELTS' is a registered trademark of its owners and is used here only to describe the exam this material helps you prepare for. No band score is guaranteed."*

**Cosa evitare:** loghi ufficiali IELTS, frasi come "official", "guaranteed Band 7/8", o l'uso di test/materiali protetti da copyright. Usa **domande e model answer originali** (creati da te/AI).

**Refund policy:**
> *"Digital product. If it's not a fit, email [your email] within 30 days for a full refund."*

## 4. Checklist revisione esperto (consigliata prima di vendere)

> *Nota:* far rivedere il kit a un **tutor IELTS certificato o ex esaminatore** alza credibilità e accuratezza. Chiedi:

- [ ] I **model answer** sono realmente di livello Band 8–9 e privi di errori?
- [ ] I **template** riflettono correttamente i **band descriptor** ufficiali (Task Achievement, Coherence, Lexical, Grammar)?
- [ ] L'**overview** in Task 1 e la **posizione** in Task 2 sono spiegati correttamente?
- [ ] La **phrase bank** evita frasi "da modello" penalizzate se usate meccanicamente?
- [ ] Tutte le **domande di pratica** sono originali (niente materiale ufficiale protetto)?

## 6. Metriche e cancello decisionale

- **Opt-in 25–30%+** = gancio ok.
- **3–10 vendite** dai primi ~150–300 lead = espandi.
- **Email→vendita 2–5%** realistico.
- Opt-in alto + 0 vendite → sistema offerta/prezzo. Opt-in basso → cambia gancio/traffico.

**Pre-sell (minimo rischio):** pubblica la sales page con "Pre-order — delivered in 7 days" prima di finire il prodotto.
