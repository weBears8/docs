## 3. Reel/TikTok/Shorts — 7 script (15–25 sec)

1. **Pain:** "Stuck at IELTS Writing 6.5? It's probably structure. Here's the 4-line intro examiners reward. Free skeleton in bio."
2. **Task 1:** "Write the OVERVIEW first — the 2 biggest trends, no numbers. That one line is a Band 7 requirement most people skip."
3. **Template:** "One structure for ANY Task 2 topic: paraphrase + position → reason + example → reason + example → restate."
4. **Paraphrase:** "Stop copying the question. Paraphrase in 4 moves: synonyms, word class, voice, order."
5. **Rubric:** "Mark your own essay like an examiner — score 4 things 0–9 and fix the lowest."
6. **Grammar:** "3 small grammar errors keeping you at 6.5 (and the fix)."
7. **Result + CTA:** "From 6.5 to 7.5 with templates + a plan. Free skeleton in bio."
