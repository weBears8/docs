## 1. Lead magnet (PDF gratuito)

**Cover:** **The 3-Step Threshold Trick** — stop your dog's reactive outburst *before* it starts, without force.

**Intro:** If your dog barks and lunges, you're not failing and your dog isn't "bad." Reactivity is fear. You can change how your dog feels with kind, force-free steps — starting with one idea most owners miss: the **threshold**.

- **Step 1 — Find the threshold:** the distance where your dog notices a trigger but can still eat and look at you. *Action:* note that distance on your next walk.
- **Step 2 — Stay under it:** every blow-up sets you back. Add distance, pick quiet routes — keep your dog in the zone where learning happens.
- **Step 3 — Pay the trigger:** under threshold, the moment your dog notices the trigger, mark ("yes") and feed several treats. Trigger → treats. Repeat. The panic fades (counter-conditioning).

**Bonus inside:** a printable **"under-threshold distance" tracker card**.
