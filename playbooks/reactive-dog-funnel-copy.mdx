## 1. Lead magnet (testo completo del PDF gratuito)

> *Nota:* impagina in Canva come PDF di 4–6 pagine.

**Cover:**
> **The 3-Step Threshold Trick**
> Stop your dog's reactive outburst *before* it starts — without force.

**Intro:**
> If your dog barks and lunges at other dogs, people, or bikes, you're not failing — and your dog isn't "bad." Reactivity is fear or over-arousal. The good news: you can change how your dog feels, using kind, force-free steps. It starts with one idea most owners miss: the **threshold**.

**Step 1 — Find the threshold.**
> Your dog's threshold is the distance at which they *notice* a trigger but can still eat a treat and look at you. Closer than that = "over threshold" = the brain floods and learning stops. *Action:* next walk, note how far away a dog can be before yours reacts. That distance is your starting line.

**Step 2 — Stay under it.**
> Every full blow-up sets you back days. So **manage**: cross the street, add distance, pick quiet routes. You're not avoiding forever — you're keeping your dog in the zone where they can learn.

**Step 3 — Pay the trigger.**
> Under threshold, the moment your dog notices the trigger, mark ("yes") and feed several treats. Trigger appears → treats happen. Repeat. Over time the trigger starts to predict good things, and the panic fades. This is **counter-conditioning** — the core of every force-free reactivity program.
