> **Introduction:** "It is often argued that [paraphrase the topic]. While some believe [view A], this essay will argue that [your position]."

> **Body 2:** "Another important factor is that [point]. [Explain]. For example, [example]. Therefore, [link back]."
> **Conclusion:** "In conclusion, although [other view], the evidence suggests that [your position], mainly because [reason 1] and [reason 2]."
